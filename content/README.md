# Phrasebook Pro editorial source

`phrasebook-pro-1000-source.csv` contains the first 1,000 lemmas from the **Spoken Georgian Frequency List v2026.07** by EasyGeorgian Research (Lasse N. and Tamar N.), licensed under CC BY 4.0.

Source: https://easygeorgian.com/spoken-georgian-frequency/

This is the Pro catalog's evidence-based editorial foundation: modern conversational Georgian, not the old written/Wikipedia lists that over-rank formal and political vocabulary. The file includes Georgian lemmas, transliteration, English glosses, part of speech, frequency, register balance, and collapsed spoken forms.

Publication gate: entries must receive a learner-friendly meaning split, Russian translation, example sentence, and native-speaker review before being promoted to the public `phrases` table. This prevents ambiguous entries (for example one spelling with two meanings) or raw corpus artifacts from becoming paid learning content.
